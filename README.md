# CS 250 – Software Development Lifecycle

## Sprint Review and Retrospective

This repository contains my Sprint Review and Retrospective project from CS 250 at Southern New Hampshire University. The project focuses on Agile development, Scrum practices, and the SNHU Travel project.

### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by understanding what the customer wants the program to do and breaking those requirements into smaller tasks. User stories help developers understand what features are needed from the user's perspective. They also help teams prioritize work and make sure the final product meets customer expectations.

### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by breaking larger projects into smaller, manageable tasks. I think Agile practices such as sprint planning, daily Scrum meetings, and sprint reviews are helpful because they keep teams organized and allow changes throughout development. I hope to use these practices in future projects to improve communication and catch problems early.

### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, completing assigned work, and being willing to help others. Software development requires teamwork between developers, testers, and other team members. I believe being reliable, accepting feedback, and working toward shared goals are important parts of being a successful team member.
